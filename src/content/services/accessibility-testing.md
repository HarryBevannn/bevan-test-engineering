## The problem

Accessibility issues exclude real users and create legal risk, and they're far cheaper to fix early than after launch.

## How I help

I combine automated scanning with manual testing using assistive technology to find the issues that tools alone miss, then help your team fix them and prevent regressions.

## What you get

- A prioritised audit report against WCAG 2.2 AA
- Automated checks added to your pipeline
- Developer-friendly fix guidance
