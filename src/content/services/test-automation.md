## The problem

Automated suites that are slow, flaky or hard to maintain end up ignored, and then they're worse than having no automation at all.

## How I help

I design and build automation frameworks that are maintainable by your team, run in your pipeline, and give fast, trustworthy feedback. If you already have a suite, I can audit it, fix the flakiness and get it back to green.

## What you get

- An automation framework that fits your stack
- Tests running on every pull request
- Documentation and handover so your team can own it
