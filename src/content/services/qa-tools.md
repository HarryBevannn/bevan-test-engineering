## The problem

In larger organisations, QA teams lose a surprising amount of time to repetitive work that isn't testing: writing up test cases in the same format again and again, creating test data by hand, chasing results across different systems, and checking whether an environment is even working before testing can start. Multiply that across a whole QA function and it adds up to a lot of time and money.

## Tools built for your team

I design and build internal tools that remove that friction, tailored to the way your business works and the tools you already use.

### Test Case Generator

Turn requirements, user stories or acceptance criteria into well-structured, consistent test cases in a fraction of the time. The generator follows your team's templates and conventions, highlights edge cases and negative scenarios that are easy to miss, and can export straight into your test management tool.

### Other tools I can build

- **Test data generators:** realistic, repeatable test data on demand, without copying production data.
- **Quality dashboards:** a single view of test results, coverage and defect trends across teams and pipelines.
- **Environment health checks:** quick automated checks that confirm an environment is up and ready before anyone starts testing.
- **Workflow automation:** small utilities that take repetitive admin off your testers' plates.

## Why it's worth it

- **Efficiency:** hours saved per tester per week soon add up across a QA department.
- **Consistency:** every team writes and reports tests the same way, which makes quality easier to measure.
- **Better coverage:** tools that suggest edge cases help teams test things they might otherwise miss.
- **Built to fit:** unlike off-the-shelf products, the tool works the way your organisation already does.
