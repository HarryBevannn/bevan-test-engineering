## The problem

Many teams test a lot but still get surprised in production. Effort goes to the wrong places, regression cycles drag on, and no one is quite sure what "done" means for quality.

## How I help

I start by understanding your product, architecture and delivery process, then identify where the real risks are. From there, we build a test strategy your team can actually follow: the right mix of unit, API, end-to-end and exploratory testing, with clear ownership and quality gates built into your pipeline.

## What you get

- A written assessment of your current testing and its gaps
- A prioritised, risk-based test strategy
- Practical recommendations you can start on straight away
